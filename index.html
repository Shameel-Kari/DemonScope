    How levels are rated

    Difficulty

    Overall difficulty from 1 to 10. Easy sits near the bottom, Extreme near the top.

    Decoration

    Visual quality: effects, color use, and how readable the level stays.

    Length

    Time for one full run, shown in minutes and seconds.

    Gameplay

    How fun, original, and well-flowing the layout is.

    Nerve

    How much composure the level demands, especially near the end.

    Memory

    How much you must learn: hidden objects and parts you can’t react to.
